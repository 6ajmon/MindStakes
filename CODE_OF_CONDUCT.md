# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want MindStakes to be a project where anyone can contribute, regardless of experience, background, identity or language. We pledge to make participation harassment-free and welcoming for everybody.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Critique code, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Use clear, neutral language, and remember that many contributors do not speak English natively.
- Respect that this is a hobby project maintained in spare time: reviews may take a while.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory comments or imagery, including slurs and sexualized content.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or pull requests.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and any other channel where you represent the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through the contact options on [github.com/6ajmon](https://github.com/6ajmon). Reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening or harmful.
